# J_Tag_Events 🎉

**J_Tag_Events** is a web platform designed to help people organize and make their events unforgettable. From parties to corporate events, we offer a wide range of specialized services and equipment for every type of event. Whether you need foam cannons, high-quality sound, spectacular lighting, or special effects, J_Tag_Events has what you need. Our mission is to provide innovative and quality solutions to make each event unique and tailored to our clients' specific needs. ✨

## Features 🚀

- **Service Catalog**: We offer a variety of services for events, including sound equipment, lighting, special effects, and more. 🎶💡
- **Contact Form**: Users can submit inquiries and request more information about our services. 📩
- **Home Page**: An attractive interface with animations and a responsive design. 🖥️📱

## Technologies Used 💻

- **HTML5** for the page structure. 🏗️
- **CSS3** for styling. 🎨
- **JavaScript** for dynamic interactions. ⚡
- **Responsive Design** to ensure the page looks great on any device. 📲
